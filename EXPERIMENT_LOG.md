| Period in 2026 | Research question and records |
| --- | --- |
| Nov 19–23 | [Teacher traces and transfer](docs/log/teacher-transfer.md). The student could copy verified teacher demonstrations. On other sources, the advantage varied with the task and how responses were scored. |
| Nov 16–18 | [Student baselines and teacher supervision](docs/log/teacher-baselines.md). Paired answer-only and reasoning experiments established what the student could already solve, which teacher examples qualified, and how often the trained models retained earlier skills. |

| Oct 24–31 | [Fixed SFT, output format and retention](docs/log/fixed-sft.md). Qwen3-8B improved on an internal development set. Changes in command format, label quality and the mix of training examples motivated checks on utility and unseen tools. |
| Oct 21–23 and earlier | [Benchmark selection and engineering pilots](docs/log/benchmark-pilots.md). Early pilots compared benchmark interfaces and documented model access, data provenance and scoring. The first experiments established which parts of the pipeline could be exercised locally. |
